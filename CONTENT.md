Trade Compliance & Export Documentation Services
What We Do For You

1. DGFT Approvals & Export Licensing

We take the hassle out of dealing with the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) by handling your registrations, incentive applications, and licenses.

    Import Export Code (IEC): Instant registration, updates, and profile maintenance.

    Incentives & Schemes: End-to-end management of EPCG, Advance Authorization, RoDTEP, RoSCTL, and Duty Drawback.

    RCMC & Registration: Seamless filing across various Export Promotion Councils.

2. Export Inspection Agency (EIA) Support

We help you secure official pre-shipment quality certifications so your products meet international market entry rules.

    Health, Sanitary, and Phytosanitary Certificates for food, agricultural, and industrial cargo.

    Preferential & Non-Preferential Certificates of Origin (COO).

    Pre-shipment quality inspection clearances required by foreign customs authorities.

3. Coir Board Registration & Licensing

Are you exporting natural coir fiber, yarn, mats, or coir pith? We handle your industry-specific legalities.

    Official Coir Board Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC).

    Export licensing, quality endorsements, and permit processing.

    Complete paperwork to claim government subsidies and export incentives for coir.

4. Textile Committee Certification

We get your fabrics, apparel, and technical textiles verified and cleared for seamless overseas distribution.

    Textile Committee registration and export endorsements.

    Pre-shipment fabric testing reports and quality certificates.

    Generalized System of Preferences (GSP) Certificates of Origin.

5. EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) Compliance

We manage your environmental compliance from start to finish so your imports and packaging stay legal.

    CPCB / SPCB Registration: Comprehensive setup for plastic packaging, e-waste, batteries, and tires.

    Target Management: Annual return filing, recycling target fulfillment, and credit transfers.

    Import Clearance: Ensuring smooth port release without environmental compliance holds.

6. BIS Certification & Product Standardization

We make sure your goods conform to mandatory quality and safety standards before they hit domestic or foreign markets.

    Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) registration & ISI mark approvals.

    Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) & Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS).

    Coordination for lab testing, product sample verification, and audit support.

7. Legalized Export Documentation & Consular Attestation

We fast-track the legalization of your commercial documents so you get paid faster and clear foreign customs effortlessly.

    Chamber of Commerce: Stamping and verification of commercial invoices and packing lists.

    Government Attestation: Apostille and Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) attestations.

    Embassy Legalization: Complete consular legalization for foreign buyers and Letter of Credit (L/C) compliance.
